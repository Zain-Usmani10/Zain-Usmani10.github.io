Software to silicon

Transformers don't synthesize well; pure convolutions do. The depth network was shrunk, quantized and compiled into hardware RTL so a gate count could be estimated for a custom chip.

Bringing up the Kria KV260

The on-robot version: a 13 MP camera feeding an FPGA depth accelerator and a CPU-side tracker at the same time.

One buffer, two consumers

Containerized an Ubuntu 22.04 workspace in Docker to bring up the board, then captured raw 13 MP NV12 streams from the AR1335 MIPI sensor through GStreamer over IAS 0.

NV12 already stores luminance as its own plane. My C++ memory fork routes that plane straight to the CPU for SVO tracking, while only the FPGA's copy pays for RGB conversion.

ML and Data Research Intern · Micro-Nano Mechatronics Lab, Windsor · May – December 2025
